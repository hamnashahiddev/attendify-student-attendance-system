# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **Attendify — Student Attendance Management System** pledge to make participation in our project and community a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to providing a positive environment regardless of age, background, experience level, education, nationality, personal appearance, or any other personal characteristic.

We pledge to interact in ways that contribute to an open, supportive, inclusive, and collaborative community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful, kind, and considerate toward others.
- Respecting different opinions, perspectives, and experiences.
- Providing constructive feedback and accepting it gracefully.
- Supporting beginners and helping others learn.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Prioritizing the well-being and success of the project community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, offensive, or derogatory comments.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Unwelcome advances or inappropriate language.
- Any behavior considered unprofessional or harmful within the project community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct.

They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior that is inappropriate, offensive, harmful, or threatening.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Contributions to project documentation
- Any public space where an individual represents Attendify or its project community

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may apply the following community impact guidelines:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Minor inappropriate behavior or unprofessional language.

**Consequence:** A private warning and clarification regarding the behavior.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or repeated inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A formal warning and restrictions on interaction with affected individuals for a specified period.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** Serious or repeated violations of community standards.

**Consequence:** Temporary suspension from project interactions, contributions, or communication.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** A repeated pattern of violations or severe misconduct.

**Consequence:** Permanent restriction from participation in the project community.
